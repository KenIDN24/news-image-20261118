## Lead

**Kuwait, Bahrain say they downed Iranian drones** — Iran's army launched
combat drones at US Patriot systems at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait and at
targets in Bahrain after US fighter jets struck an IRGC missile site on Iran's
Qeshm Island. Kuwait's General Staff confirmed intercepting hostile UAVs in
the early hours of 18 November; Bahrain said it intercepted further attacks
and accused Tehran of a "systematic hostile approach".
— AP reporting summarised by Pollar News (Middle East thread, 18 Nov);
Reuters (US official on the Qeshm strike); Al Jazeera, CBS News and the
Japan Times via One Turn News' 18 Nov edition; Xinhua/Anadolu via House of
Saud Daily Briefing, 18 Nov.

## Panels

**Ukraine destroys Crimea bridge road span** — Ukrainian forces struck the
Kerch Bridge overnight 16–17 November, destroying a metal arch and the
automobile (road) span; the attack continued into a second day. Russia
launched 141 drones at Ukraine overnight into 18 November; air defences
neutralised 126.
— AP via DevDiscourse (18 Nov: "after Ukraine destroyed the vital Kerch road
bridge"); Ukrinform/h.ua 18 Nov air-attacks brief (7–10 sources);
One Turn News, 18 Nov.

**Peltola wins Alaska; Senate 56–44** — Democrat Mary Peltola defeated
two-term Republican senator Dan Sullivan in Alaska, the last Senate race
called in the midterms (AP, 18 November). The win gives Democrats a 56–44
majority after nine flips, the most since 1958; Peltola is the first Alaska
Native elected to the US Senate.
— Associated Press, 18 Nov 2026 (via Election Law Blog and The Sen Times);
USA Today, 18 Nov; CNN/NBC projections via KTBB/AP.

**Xi opens APEC summit in Shenzhen** — Xi Jinping opened the APEC CEO Summit
in Shenzhen on Wednesday with a keynote pledging free and open trade ("openness
brings progress"). Putin spoke at the opening; Trump arrived Wednesday after
meeting President Marcos in Manila. The three leaders' trilateral summit is
scheduled for Thursday.
— Reuters (Trump–Marcos Manila, and APEC talking points); AP via DevDiscourse
and UAN World, 18 Nov; Seoul Economic Daily (EPA/Yonhap photo), 18 Nov;
Le Monde, 18 Nov.

**Congo Ebola passes 11,000 cases, 5,400 deaths** — WHO said the Bundibugyo
Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has more than doubled
in three months, with more than 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths across 67 health
zones in eight provinces; children are "paying a terrible price". There is no
licensed vaccine or treatment for the Bundibugyo strain.
— UN News "World News in Brief", 18 Nov 2026; WHO via Health Signal Africa,
18 Nov briefing; News Central TV (Reuters), 18 Nov.

**US loses measles-free status after 26 years** — The Pan American Health
Organization announced measles is no longer eliminated in the United States,
ending a status held since 2000, after more than 5,400 cases and 10 deaths
reported in 2026.
— Horizon news-and-markets digest, 18 Nov (2 sources); CNA via FeedCast Daily
Brief, 18 Nov; The Guardian explainer via VaaWaa headlines, 18 Nov.

**Nvidia revenue doubles to $115.8bn** — Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of
$115.84 billion, more than double a year earlier and above the $109.1 billion
forecast (EPS $2.68 vs $2.48 expected), guided to about $130 billion for the
current quarter, and its board said it would not reinstate co-founder Jensen
Huang as CEO after he stepped down.
— Saxo Bank "London Quick Take", 18 Nov; Sharecast, 18 Nov; XTB, 18 Nov;
Reuters podcast brief, 18 Nov.

**GTA VI goes on sale after a 13-year wait** — Grand Theft Auto VI, the first
new GTA since 2013, launches at midnight local time territory by territory,
unlocking first in New Zealand (11:00 GMT Wednesday); midnight launches and
fan events ran through Wednesday 18 November.
— AFP via The Business Standard (TBS News), 18 Nov; Le Monde, 18 Nov;
Reuters podcast brief, 18 Nov.
